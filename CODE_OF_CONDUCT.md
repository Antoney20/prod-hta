# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

BPTAP is a public health platform developed to serve the people of Kenya and support evidence-based health policy. Everyone who contributes to, uses, or engages with this project is expected to uphold the standards outlined here.

We are committed to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and professional environment for all participants — regardless of background, experience level, nationality, or affiliation.

---

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Give and receive feedback professionally
- Focus on what is best for the platform and the communities it serves
- Acknowledge differing viewpoints and engage in good faith
- Show consideration for the public health context this project operates in

---

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind
- Sharing private or sensitive information without consent
- Deliberately submitting false, misleading, or harmful proposals or data
- Attempting to disrupt, exploit, or misuse the platform
- Any conduct that could damage public trust in Kenya's health systems

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — including GitHub discussions, issues, pull requests, and any communication related to BPTAP — as well as public representations of the project.

---

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the CEMA team at:

**📧 hbtap@uonbi.ac.ke**

All reports will be reviewed confidentially. Violations may result in removal from the project or restricted access to the platform.
